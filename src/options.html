A tab group titled --- is the barrier. Tabs to its left are regular tabs, and tabs to its right are sticky. Any group titled with only dashes counts, like - or —.

Applies to tabs opened from other apps, other windows, the + button, pinned tabs, and sticky tabs. Tabs opened from regular tabs stay where Chrome puts them.

Leave empty or set to 0 to never close tabs. Pinned, active and sticky tabs are never closed. With no --- group, nothing is closed.

The "Copy the current tab's URL" shortcut needs permission to write to the clipboard. Chrome asks the first time you use it, or you can allow it here: Allowed.

To change keyboard shortcuts, go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
